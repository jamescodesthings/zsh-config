# j reorder

> Edit the top entries as a list of paths in your editor; the saved order sets their ranks.
> Lines that are not existing entries are dropped.

# Options

- `--count N`: how many top entries to edit (default 10)

# Examples

- Reorder the top 10:

`j reorder`

- Reorder the top N:

`j reorder --count 20`
